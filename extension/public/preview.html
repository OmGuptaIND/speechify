MAKE ROOM FOR A GOOD READ

A voice to keep you company.

Sample article · About a minute

Sometimes a familiar voice makes it easier to stay with a story. You can listen to the words, follow the highlight, or do a little of both. There is no need to keep the same pace as anyone else.

Keep your place

Click a sentence and start right there. When something deserves a second listen, go back to that paragraph. The words on the page stay where they belong, and your place stays with you.

Take it at your own pace

Try a slower speed for a complicated idea, or move a little faster through familiar ground. You can save a passage that matters and come back when you have more time.

Little things add up

A comfortable voice, a gentle highlight, and controls within reach can make a long article feel more approachable. Start with one paragraph and see where it takes you.
